2. Icons drawn by Oeca.exe
==============================================================================

Oeca draws these icons from vector outlines built into Oeca.exe, converted
from the original icon artwork.

Clipboard, House blank, Music-alt, Palette, Info, Settings and Trash icons:
  designed by UIcons from Flaticon, https://www.flaticon.com/uicons
  Used under Flaticon's free license (free for commercial use with
  attribution), which allows altering them.

Drop page icon:
  "Inbox" (Noun Project icon 1052548), https://thenounproject.com/icon/1052548/
  Created by Jivan from the Noun Project
  Used under the Noun Project free license, with attribution.
