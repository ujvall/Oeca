==============================================================================

Official builds of Oeca.exe embed the General Sans typeface (Regular, Medium
and Semibold), unmodified, to draw their text:

  https://www.fontshare.com/fonts/general-sans

General Sans and its name are the property of the Indian Type Foundry (ITF),
