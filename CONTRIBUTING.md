# Contributing to Oeca

Oeca is source-available, proprietary software (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)), developed by one person.

**Contributions are not being accepted at the moment.** Please do not open pull requests or send code, artwork or other material: there is no contributor agreement, so they cannot be merged.

Bug reports and feature requests are welcome in [Issues](https://github.com/ujvall/Oeca/issues). Opening an issue does not grant you any rights in Oeca and does not make you a contributor to it.
